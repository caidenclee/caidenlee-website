About Me

My Story So Far.

In 2020 I picked up a Rubik's Cube for the first time and spent about three minutes solving it. Curious, I went online to see how fast people could actually solve one, and I came across Feliks Zemdegs solving a cube in 4.22 seconds. I was completely blown away. That moment changed my life.

By 2021 I was deep into speedcubing, and in 2022 I competed in my first WCA competition and launched my YouTube channel with one mission: to change the cubing space through coaching and content. A year and a half ago I started coaching full time and have since taught over 100 people to solve a cube. In 2025 I hit my first sub-7 official average using the full ZB method and currently sit at #37 in the US. Next up: a sub-6 official average and a sub-5 single. The story's just getting started.
